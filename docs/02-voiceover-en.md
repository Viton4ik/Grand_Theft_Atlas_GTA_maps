# GRAND THEFT ATLAS - How GTA's Maps Grew Up
## Voice-over script (EN) · draft v1

**Narrator:** Kokoro `bm_george` - dry British documentary voice that is secretly enjoying itself.
**Runtime target:** ~9:05 (chapter 09, GTA VI, is a placeholder until the game ships on 19 Nov 2026).
**Pace:** ~145 wpm. Timecodes are targets; the final timeline is built from the real length of each synthesized line, so picture follows voice, not the other way round.

Notation: `[pause]` ≈ 0.5 s · `[beat]` ≈ 1 s comic pause · *italics* = stress.

Running gags:
- **The km² counter** in the corner. It only ever goes up... until GTA IV.
- **The Excuse Board.** Every game invents a new reason why you can't cross the bridge yet. Each one gets stamped onto a growing list.

---

### 00 · COLD OPEN (0:00 - 0:30)
**0:01** - (quiet, close)
This is Liberty City in 1997. [pause] All of it. [beat] You could see the whole thing from a helicopter that didn't exist yet.

**0:09** - (camera pulls out, and out, and out)
Twenty-nine years later, the map is so big it needs its own weather, its own wildlife… [pause] and its own state.

**0:18** - (title)
This is the story of how a few hundred pixels of New York became an entire fake Florida. [beat] Grand Theft Atlas.

### 01 · GTA (1997): THE VIEW FROM ABOVE (0:30 - 1:10)
**0:31**
The first Grand Theft Auto came out of a small Scottish studio, DMA Design, in 1997. Three cities, each one a real place wearing a fake moustache.

**0:39** - (pins drop on the real map)
Liberty City was New York. [pause] San Andreas, San Francisco. [pause] Vice City, Miami.

**0:45** - (deadpan)
You saw it all from straight above, like a pigeon with a criminal record. There was no minimap. There was an arrow. [beat] The arrow believed in you.

**0:57**
And some streets stayed locked until you'd done enough crime to deserve them. [pause] Remember that idea. It's coming back. [beat] Many, many times.

### 02 · LONDON (1999): THE ONLY REAL CITY (1:10 - 1:40)
**1:11**
Then GTA did something it has never done since. [pause] It went to a real city, and used its real name.

**1:18**
London, 1969. Piccadilly Circus, real street names, and driving on the left. [beat] Which is still the most dangerous mission in the series.

**1:27**
A free add-on, London 1961, went back eight years earlier and even slipped in a multiplayer map of Manchester. [pause] Rain not included. Rain implied.

### 03 · GTA 2 (1999): ANYWHERE, USA (1:40 - 2:10)
**1:41**
GTA 2 went the other way: a city with no real-world twin at all. [pause] Its name was literally "Anywhere."

**1:49**
A retro-future metropolis in three districts - Downtown, Residential and Industrial - each with its own gangs, its own radio… and its own reasons to shoot you.

**1:59** - (searching pins pop up all over the US map)
So where *is* Anywhere? [beat] Exactly.

### 04 · GTA III (2001): THE CAMERA DROPS (2:10 - 3:05)
**2:11** - (the flat map tilts up into 3D)
In 2001, the camera came down from the sky and landed behind your shoulder. [beat] And everything changed.

**2:19**
Liberty City returned in full 3D, built across three islands: Portland, Staunton Island and Shoreside Vale.

**2:27**
But you couldn't go everywhere at once. The bridge to Staunton had just been blown up. [pause] The bridge to Shoreside was "broken." [pause] The tunnel was "still under construction."

**2:39** - (stamp: EXCUSE #1)
This is the birth of the great GTA tradition: [beat] the closed bridge. Rockstar would spend the next twenty-five years inventing reasons for it.

**2:49**
And for the first time, a radar sat in the corner of the screen, telling you where you were. [pause] Not where to go. Just where you were. [beat] Navigation was still your problem.

**2:57** - (km² counter appears)
Total area: about eight square kilometres. Remember the number.

### 05 · VICE CITY (2002): NEON & HURRICANES (3:05 - 3:50)
**3:06** - (synthwave kicks in)
One year later: Miami, 1986. Or, as the game calls it, Vice City.

**3:12**
Two big islands, a bay full of millionaires' little islands in between, and pastel art-deco hotels along the beach.

**3:20**
You start on the beach side, because every bridge to the mainland is closed. [pause] Why? [beat] Hurricane Hermione.

**3:28** - (stamp: EXCUSE #2)
Excuse number two: weather.

**3:31**
No trains, no subway. Just bridges, boats, and a lot of very fast cars with very loud stereos.

**3:39** - (counter ticks: 9.1)
At about nine square kilometres, it was barely bigger than Liberty City. [pause] The next step would not be small.

### 06 · SAN ANDREAS (2004): A WHOLE STATE (3:50 - 4:55)
**3:51** - (camera flies west, like a plane)
In 2004, GTA stopped making cities and started making states.

**3:56**
San Andreas had three of them: Los Santos, for Los Angeles… [pause] San Fierro, for San Francisco… [pause] and Las Venturas, for Las Vegas.

**4:06**
And between the cities, for the first time, there was *nothing*. [beat] Glorious nothing. Forests, farms, desert, a dam, a giant radio telescope, and a mountain called Chiliad that every player has fallen off at least once.

**4:20** - (real California, Nevada and Arizona squeeze into one small island)
A slice of three real states was squeezed into one island you could drive across in a few minutes.

**4:27** - (stamp: EXCUSE #3)
And the locked zones? This time, no hurricane. Cross the border too early and the police simply decided you'd committed a crime. [beat] Excuse number three: "because we said so."

**4:39**
San Andreas also let you put your own marker on the map for the first time. [pause] Getting there was still up to you.

**4:46** - (counter jumps to 38.2)
Thirty-eight square kilometres. Four times bigger than Vice City.

### 07 · GTA IV (2008): SMALLER, TALLER, DENSER (4:55 - 6:00)
**4:56** - (everything goes grey-steel; counter ticks DOWN, sad trombone)
And then, for the first time in GTA history, the map got smaller.

**5:02**
GTA IV threw out the old Liberty City and rebuilt it from scratch on a brand-new engine. Only about sixteen square kilometres - less than half of San Andreas.

**5:11** - (blocks of Algonquin rise into skyscrapers)
But it grew upwards. Broker, for Brooklyn. Dukes, for Queens. Bohan, for the Bronx. Algonquin, for Manhattan. And across the river, Alderney - New Jersey - as a separate state.

**5:24**
It was the most New York that New York had ever been in a game. Except for Staten Island, which simply… wasn't invited.

**5:31** - (stamp: EXCUSE #4)
The bridges? Closed, of course. [beat] Terror alert. Excuse number four.

**5:37** - (a GPS line draws itself through the streets)
But the real revolution was in the corner of the screen. Set a waypoint, and GPS drew the route for you, and recalculated it when you ignored it. In the fancy cars, it even talked.

**5:50**
A year later, Chinatown Wars shrank this same city onto a Nintendo DS, and quietly cut Alderney off the map.

### 08 · GTA V (2013): THE BIG ONE (6:00 - 7:10)
**6:01** - (back west, bright sunshine palette)
In 2013, GTA went back to Los Santos. [pause] But not the old one.

**6:06**
GTA V rebuilt southern San Andreas: the city of Los Santos and the wide-open Blaine County. Mount Chiliad came back. A salty inland sea, a desert, a military base, and a coast full of very expensive houses with very bad neighbours.

**6:20** - (camera dives under water)
And then it added a new direction: down. The entire ocean floor is modelled, with wrecks, caves… and things you should probably not be swimming next to.

**6:31** - (counter jumps to 75.8)
Total area: almost seventy-six square kilometres. More than nine Liberty Cities from GTA III.

**6:39** - (real Los Angeles outline slides under the map)
And still, the real city of Los Angeles alone is about seventeen times bigger. [beat] Rockstar doesn't build cities. It builds the highlights.

**6:49** - (two satellite islands pop up)
GTA V even had satellite maps: a snowy prologue in North Yankton… and, seven years later, Cayo Perico - the first new island ever added to GTA Online.

**7:01**
For more than a decade, this was the biggest GTA map ever made. [pause] Until now.

### 09 · GTA VI (2026): LEONIDA (7:10 - 8:05) ⏸ PLACEHOLDER
**7:11** - (Florida outline, six regions light up one by one)
On the nineteenth of November 2026, GTA returns to Vice City. [pause] But this time, Vice City is just the beginning.

**7:19**
The state of Leonida - Rockstar's Florida - with six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, the Grassrivers swamps, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga up on the northern border.

**7:32**

### 10 · THE SCOREBOARD (8:05 - 8:45)
**8:06** - (all maps side by side, same scale)
So here they all are, side by side, at the same scale.

**8:11**
From eight square kilometres to seventy-six… with one dramatic dip in the middle, when Rockstar decided that taller beats wider.

**8:20**
The camera went from the sky, to the shoulder, to the bottom of the ocean. The arrow became a radar, the radar became a GPS. [beat] And the bridges? [pause] They're still closed. They just come up with better excuses.

**8:33**

### 11 · OUTRO (8:45 - 9:05)
**8:46**
Every GTA map is a love letter to a real place - written by someone who's a bit too fond of explosions.

**8:54** - (zoom back to the tiny 1997 square)
And it all started here. [pause] A few hundred pixels… and an arrow that believed in you.
